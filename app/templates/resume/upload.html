How NLP Parsing Works

JobHelper uses a multi-stage parsing engine to extract structured data from unstructured resumes:

  • Text Extraction: PyPDF for multi-page PDF documents & python-docx for DOCX tables and paragraphs.
  • Skill Taxonomy: Identifies programming languages, frameworks, cloud, data science, and soft skills.
  • Academic & Career: Parses degree credentials, institutions, and estimates career years.
  • Quality Audit: Generates a 0-100 score analyzing quantifiable achievements and action verbs.
